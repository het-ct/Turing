Hardware overview

Het-X1 is an MCU product for the integration of display, audio, communication and local intelligence. The platform integrates 32-bit RISC-V CPU, NPU, 2.5D GPU, on-chip SRAM, and provides display, camera, audio, Bluetooth, optional Wi-Fi and rich control peripherals.

Summary of core competencies

module

illustrate

CPU

32-bit RISC-V, supports FPU, up to 180 MHz, CPU exclusive 288 KB SRAM

NPU

Up to 50 GOPS, compatible with DSP instructions, NPU exclusive 480 KB SRAM

GPU

2.5D graphics acceleration, 3×3 Matrix, JPEG/GIF encoding and decoding related capabilities

show

8080, DSPI/QSPI/SPI, RGB565, specific capabilities are limited by packaging and screen driver

Camera

SPI-CSI; QFN68 can provide DVP, and the support range is subject to the adapted driver.

Audio

Dual DAC, dual microphone ADC, EQ and some voice pre-processing capabilities

communication

BR/EDR, BLE, optional Wi-Fi, USB, UART, SPI, IIC, IR

The storage and interface resources of QFN32 and QFN68 are different. When selecting, you need to check the display, camera, PSRAM/ROM, GPIO quantity and PCB process at the same time.
