Critical Materials and AVL

AVL (Approved Vendor List) is used to restrict key devices that have completed electrical, drive and supply verification. The selection should not only look at the interface name, but also match the voltage, timing, bandwidth, structural size, software driver and life cycle.

Device families prioritized for verification

Display and touch, Camera, IMU, microphone and speaker, motor and encoder, Flash/SD, wireless module and power supply device.

Display and touch

  • TFT/OLED: Confirm resolution, pixel format, SPI/QSPI/8080/RGB interface, refresh rate, TE, backlight power and initialization sequence.

  • Touch Controller: Confirm I2C/SPI address, reset/interrupt level, coordinate direction, edge linearity and cover thickness.

  • Structural Materials: Color film, PEDOT and cover plates need to be verified together with brightness, touch sensitivity and lamination process.

Image and Sensor

  • Camera: Distinguish between SPI-CSI and DVP, check pixel clock, output format, frame rate, lens field of view and low-light performance.

  • IMU: Confirm range, output data rate, FIFO/interrupts, power consumption, and coordinate systems supported by the algorithm library.

  • Environmental Sensors: Check for I2C address conflicts, pull-ups, power sequencing, measurement cycles, and how calibration data is saved.

Audio and Actuators

  • MIC/Speaker: Select devices by single-ended/differential/PDM interface and evaluate sensitivity, impedance, cavity, echo path and amplifier headroom.

  • Motor: Polarized motors, brushed motors and BLDC have different drive, power supply, stall current, back EMF and heat dissipation schemes.

  • Encoder: Confirm phase, number of pulses per revolution, debounce and QDEC input level, covering forward and reverse and fast rotation tests.

Admission of alternative materials

  1. Compare key electrical parameters and packages, not just device functional names.

  2. Complete driver initialization, full temperature, low voltage, EMC and long-term stress testing on the target PCB.

  3. Update BOM, driver selection, initialization parameters, calibration data and regression test records.

  4. Document vendor, revision, applicable boards, and known limitations for each approved part number.

Warning

The instruction manual only gives the material category and does not form a complete list of material numbers that can be directly used for procurement. Devices that have not been jointly verified by hardware and software should not be added to mass production AVL just because the interface is compatible.
