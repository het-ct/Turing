Low power consumption and power management

Overview

Het-X1 integrates LDO, lithium battery charging management and NVDC, and the project also provides board-level implementations such as platform/bsp/bsp_sleep.c and bsp_charge.c. Low power must jointly handle clock, display, touch, wireless, storage and wake sources.

Configuration items

Power-related configurations in config.h include BUCK_MODE_EN, SYS_CLK_SEL, SOFT_POWER_ON_OFF, LP_XOSC_CLOCK_EN, RTC_CLOCK_PDN_EN, and GUI_AUTO_POWER_EN. Different boards may have different support ranges, and the current must be measured after modification.

Enter the low power consumption process

  1. The application stops new traffic and waits for storage, audio, display, and communication operations to complete.

  2. Save necessary state, turn off backlight and unnecessary peripheral interrupts/DMA.

  3. Configure key, RTC, touch or communication wake-up sources.

  4. Call the board-level sleep entry.

  5. Resume clock, bus, display and services after wake-up and check the cause of wake-up.

Example

int app_enter_sleep(void)
{
    ui_suspend();
    storage_flush();
    bsp_sleep_prepare();
    return bsp_sleep_enter();
}

Things to note

  • Flash writing must be completed before entering sleep, and it is prohibited to update key configurations during the power-off window.

  • RTC and low-power crystal oscillator selection will affect shutdown current and timing accuracy.

  • It cannot be assumed that all peripheral registers retain their original values ​​after wake-up.

  • Typical/maximum current, allowed wake-up sources and recovery delays for each mode To be developed.

Main API

Currently, bsp_sleep.h, bsp_charge.h and engineering power control functions prevail; the unified power management API is to be developed.
