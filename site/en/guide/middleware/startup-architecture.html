Startup Architecture

: This page describes the boundaries of responsibility for MCU firmware from reset to application entry. The specific startup files, link scripts, and startup assembly are subject to the engineering platform and target project; Do not apply Android/Linux BootROM, Preloader, U-Boot, Kernel, or Init layering.

Secure Boot

Signature verification, eFuse root of trust, and startup chain are Security architecture. Currently, the manual does not support version number rollback protection; The product release process should not mistake A/B partition recovery for hardware rollback protection.

The serial port logs of the startup debug list

  • output chips, board types, firmware versions, and reset reasons during the earliest available stage.

  • When a HardFault/exception occurs, saves PC, SP, exception cause, and most recent task information.

  • Check that the startup code matches the RAM/ROM area of the linked script, and confirm that there is no overflow in the resident area.

  • Repeated validation in cold start, hot reset, low voltage, watchdog, and power-off recovery scenarios.
