Board-level Adaptation:

Board-level adaptation maps chip capabilities to actual power supplies, pins, devices, and product peripherals. The HAL page describes the unified API; this page explains how to connect them to the target board.

Compatible with sequential

  1. recording board versions, chip packaging, power rails, crystal oscillators, boot pins, and debugging serial ports.

  2. Establish a single allocation source for resources such as GPIO, UART, IIC, SPI, LCD, ADC, PWM, QDEC, and more.

  3. Initialize in the order of power, clock, GPIO multiplexing, bus, device driver, and UI/CBB.

  4. Provides deinitialization, error return, and low-power recovery paths for each resource.

  5. Cold start, reset, peripheral read/write, and long-term operation verification are completed on the target board.
