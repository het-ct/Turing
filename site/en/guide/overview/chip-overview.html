Overview of the X1 Chip

The X1 is an MCU/SoC platform designed for low-power HMI, audio, connectivity, and edge algorithm products. Chip-level capabilities must be used in specific products through board-level power, clock, pin, and driver configurations.

Platform capability

  • a 32-bit RISC-V CPU, up to 180 MHz, equipped with an interrupt controller and cache SRAM.

  • NPU/DSP is suitable for local signal processing and inference; The GPU provides 2.5D graphics and JPEG/GIF encoding/decoding capabilities.

  • Integrates peripherals such as LCD, Camera, Audio, BT/Wi-Fi (optional by model), USB, UART, SPI, IIC, Timer, PWM, ADC, QDEC, and IR.

  • On-chip/external Flash, PSRAM, SD card, and power management unit are selected according to package and board configuration.

Architecture and Storage

Complete Bus Diagram, Storage Partitioning, .com/.bank Rules, and Interrupt Placement Example See Chip architecture and memory. Areas involved in erasure must meet 4 KB alignment; Real-time interrupt paths should prioritize resident RAM.

Electrical and package

power supply, GPIO drivers, audio/RF, ambient temperature, ESD, and power-on timing are all Electrical characteristics and design limits. QFN32/QFN68 packages and core board options are available in Packages and development boards.

能力边界

  • maximum frequency, drive current, and resolution given in the capability boundary chip manual are not guaranteed values for the entire machine; temperature rise, power consumption, and PCB verification must be combined.

  • Wireless, camera, audio, and high-speed display capabilities are limited by specific packaging, boards, licensing, and driver implementations.

  • There is currently no public statement for a universal RTOS adapter layer: To be developed. Applications should follow the task/interrupt model of existing engineering.
