Critical components and AVL

AVL (Approved Vendor List) is used to restrict key components that have completed electrical, drive, and supply verification. Component selection should not only look at the interface name, but also match voltage, timing, bandwidth, structural dimensions, software drivers, and lifecycle.

Priority-Verified Device Family

Display and touch, camera, IMU, microphone and speaker, motor and encoder, flash/SD, wireless modules, and power devices.

Display and Touch

  • TFT/OLED: Confirm resolution, pixel format, SPI/QSPI/8080/RGB interface, refresh rate, TE, backlight power, and initialization sequence.

  • Touch Controller: Confirm I2C/SPI address, reset/interrupt levels, coordinate orientation, edge linearity, and cover thickness.

  • Structural Materials: The color film, PEDOT, and cover plate need to be jointly verified with brightness, touch sensitivity, and lamination process.

Images and Sensors

  • Camera: Distinguish between SPI-CSI and DVP, and check the pixel clock, output format, frame rate, lens field of view, and low-light performance.

  • IMU: Confirm the measurement range, output data rate, FIFO/interrupts, power consumption, and coordinate systems supported by the algorithm library.

  • Environmental Sensor: Check I2C address conflicts, pull-ups, power sequencing, measurement cycles, and calibration data storage methods.

Audio and actuators

  • MIC/Speaker: Select components according to single-ended/differential/PDM interface, and evaluate sensitivity, impedance, cavity, echo path, and amplifier headroom.

  • Motors: The drive, power supply, stall current, back EMF, and heat dissipation solutions differ for polarized motors, brushed motors, and BLDC motors.

  • Encoder: Confirm the phase, pulses per revolution, debounce, and QDEC input levels, covering forward/reverse and rapid rotation tests.

Alternative Material Approval

  1. Compare key electrical parameters and packaging, rather than just the device function names.

  2. Complete driver initialization, full temperature, low voltage, EMC, and long-term stress testing on the target PCB.

  3. Update BOM, driver selection, initialization parameters, calibration data, and regression test records.

  4. Record the supplier, version, applicable boards, and known limitations for each approved part number.

Warning

The manual only provides material categories and does not form a complete list of part numbers that can be directly used for procurement. Devices that have not been jointly verified by hardware and software should not be added to the mass production AVL just because of interface compatibility.
