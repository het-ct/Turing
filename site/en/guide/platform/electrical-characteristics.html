Electrical characteristics and design limits

This page summarizes the most commonly used boundary values for schematic design, device selection, and laboratory validation. Parameters are sourced from the “Turing Platform User Manual”; Before formal mass production, the datasheet and hardware release package for the corresponding chip batch should be used as the final basis.

the GPIO input voltage range is -0.3 V to VDDIO + 0.3 V. Standard GPIO drivers have a default drive capacity of 8 mA, with high-drive ports configurable up to 32 mA; Internal pull-up/pull-down options are available in 0.3 kΩ, 10 kΩ, and 200 kΩ ranges, with resistance accuracy specified in the manual at ±20%. PE0/PE1 high-voltage IO is designed only for 8 mA.

Warning

The greatest driving force is not the recommended continuous working point. Backlights, motors, speakers, and high-current LEDs must use dedicated drivers while calculating the chip’s total current, ground return, and thermal design.

Audio and RF summaries

  • DAC supports sampling rates from 8 kHz to 96 kHz; Typical SNR for single-ended mode is 103 dB, and typical SNR for differential mode is 107.5 dB.

  • Audio ADCs support differential and single-ended inputs, with typical SNR of about 97 to 98 dB, and actual performance is affected by front-end impedance, ground noise, and PCB layout.

  • a maximum transmit power of 10 dBm, and a typical 1M BLE reception sensitivity is -98.5 dBm.

  • Optional Wi-Fi operates in the 2.4 GHz band, covering 2412 MHz to 2484 MHz; RF indicators should be re-measured under the overall antenna and regulatory conditions.

Power-On Sequence

Before powering on, the input should be kept below 200 mV for at least 400 ms, then rise to normal operating range within 500 ms. Slow power rise, repeated drops, or reverse charging of the perimeter before the main power supply should all be covered and tested during the prototype stage.

Board-level verification recommends

  1. measuring VBAT/VDDIO waveforms when maximum backlight, wireless transmission, audio playback, and motor startup occur simultaneously.

  2. Check the power timing after reset, cold start, quick power-off restart, and USB plugging and unplugging.

  3. Verify whether there are any glitches during GPIO default state, pull-up error, and peripheral multiplexing switching.

  4. , audio background noise, and system stability were retested under temperature, battery low voltage, and parallel charging conditions.
